The Product · SuryaGbSAR

See failures before they happen.

SuryaGbSAR is a ground-based synthetic aperture radar that continuously scans critical assets - mines, dams, bridges - at sub-millimeter precision, day and night, in all weather, with the reliability and cost structure only possible when it's built in India.

Millimeter precision, kilometers of reach

Detects deformation as small as 0.1 mm over ranges up to 4 km - enough to catch the earliest tremor of a slope, crack, or arch long before the naked eye can see it.

24×7, day-night, all-weather

Unlike LiDAR, optical or manual surveys, radar cuts through dust, haze, fog, rain and darkness. No downtime - no monsoon blind spots, no night-shift blackouts.

50% lower cost, 3× faster scale

Made in India means nationwide deployment, not expensive pilots - with the same data fidelity as imported systems at a fraction of the lifecycle cost.

Sovereign & secure by design

All data stays in India. No foreign telemetry, no export-controlled components, no vendor-locked cloud. Designed to be auditable by national agencies.

Predictive, not reactive

ML models trained on Indian terrain convert raw movement into risk scores, automated alerts, and evacuation leads - days to weeks before a failure event.

Built for Indian conditions

Engineered for 50°C heat, dust storms, monsoon floods, patchy grid power, and long logistics cycles. Field-serviceable with parts made on-shore.

Services

Beyond the radar.

A radar watches one asset very closely. Most asset bases need the opposite - a way to keep track of thousands of dispersed assets at once. We run that too, on three layers: staff on the ground, drones overhead, satellites above both.

Asset Monitoring

Know what was actually built, and what is standing today.

A single register for every asset, reconciled to government master data, with three independent layers of observation writing to the same record - so a progress figure can be opened down to the photograph, the coordinate and the person behind it.

01

On-ground staff

Field inspection app - OTP sign-in, geo-fenced live camera capture, two teams cross-validating each other's evidence.

02

Drone-based monitoring

Orthomosaic and elevation capture of the whole site, giving a measurable record independent of what the field reported.

03

Satellite-based monitoring

Whole-portfolio coverage and change detection over time, repeatable as imagery refreshes, with no field mobilisation.

How it works
Land Encroachment Detection

Find what is standing on public land, parcel by parcel.

Satellite imagery and AI assessment at the level of the land record itself - the individual parcel - across whole regions at once, in a form precise enough to act on.

01

Parcel-accurate mapping

Every boundary from the land record mapped onto current high-resolution imagery, at the unit the owner already administers.

02

AI structure detection

Each parcel examined for permanent structures; those standing on land recorded as public are flagged for verification.

03

Reports and tracking

Print-ready reports per site and region, plus a dashboard holding occupation and lease status against the same parcel.
