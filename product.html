How It Works

From microwave pulse
to evacuation order.

A complete sense-to-decision pipeline - so operators don't get a wall of data, they get a clear next action.

Emit & receive

Phased microwave pulses scan the asset over a synthetic aperture. Range-Doppler processing creates a 2-D amplitude image.

Interferometric stack

Consecutive scans are stacked. Phase differences between pixels reveal line-of-sight motion to 1/10th of a millimeter.

Terrain & atmosphere correction

Our ML models remove noise from vegetation, humidity, thermal drift and geometric shadowing - calibrated on Indian conditions.

Predict & alert

Velocity, acceleration and clustering patterns are scored against failure signatures - alerts pushed to SCADA, WhatsApp and command dashboards.
