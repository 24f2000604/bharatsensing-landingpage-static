Where It Deploys

One radar, six jobs.

SuryaGbSAR covers an entire open-cast operation from a single trailer-mounted installation, repositioned as the mine plan evolves.

⛰️

Active bench faces

Track micro-creep on working benches in real time; call stop-work on the specific bench, not the whole mine.

🧱

Final wall & highwall

Long-range monitoring of high walls across pit dimensions - days of warning before large-scale rotational or wedge failure.

🏔️

Overburden dumps

Detect progressive settlement and base-failure risk on external waste dumps during and after monsoon.

💧

Tailings dams

Continuous TSF embankment monitoring - a globally rising regulatory concern post-Brumadinho.

🚧

Haul road stability

Identify unstable cut-slopes above critical haul roads before a rockfall blocks hauling or injures crew.

📋

Regulatory reporting

Automated DGMS monthly safety submissions, with full audit trail of every detected event and response.

A Typical Deployment

From mobilisation
to early warnings - fast.
