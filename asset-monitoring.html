Know what was built,
and what is standing
there today.

One register for every asset, with three independent layers of observation writing to the same record — so a progress figure can be opened down to the photograph, the coordinate and the person behind it.

The differentiator

Evidence that can contradict the report

A progress number travelling up a reporting chain has no way of being wrong in public. Attach a geo-tagged photograph, a timestamp and a named user to every status change, then put a drone pass and a satellite read beside it, and the number becomes checkable. Three independent layers disagree with each other when something is off — which is the entire point.

The problem

Large build programmes run at a scale no inspection team can keep up with. Progress is reported upward through the same chain that is responsible for delivering it. By the time a figure reaches leadership it has passed through several hands, and there is no evidence attached to it that anyone can check.

The question is not whether people are honest. It is that a number with no photograph, no coordinate and no timestamp behind it cannot be audited by anyone, and so cannot be acted on with confidence.

What we deliver

  • A single asset register reconciled against the authoritative source data, down to the reference code for every site
  • A field inspection application with enforced live capture, geo-fencing and OTP-only identity
  • Independent cross-validation between two separate field teams on every asset
  • Drone survey of a selected cohort, producing a measurable record independent of field reporting
  • Satellite assessment across the full portfolio, repeated as imagery refreshes
  • Dashboards scoped to each team’s own area, with pendency that does not quietly disappear
  • Automated reminders and escalation on a registered-template messaging pipeline
  • Every report exportable at whatever level it is being viewed

The drone and satellite layers are not reported by the people being measured. Where they disagree with the field record, the disagreement is the finding worth chasing.

The layers

Three observations.
One record.

Each layer is cheaper and wider than the one before it, and less detailed. Together they cover the portfolio without anyone pretending a single method could.

LAYER 01

On-ground staff

Field teams capture what is actually standing, on a mobile app built for low connectivity and low trust. Live-camera capture only, inside a geo-fence around the asset, every photograph geo-tagged and tied to a named user who signed in by one-time password. Two independent teams inspect the same asset and validate each other’s evidence before a supervisor records a verdict.

OTP-only sign-inLive capture, geo-fenced Two-team cross-validationAccess scoped to own area
LAYER 02

Drone-based monitoring

Where ground photographs cannot show the whole asset, we fly it. Orthomosaic and elevation capture gives a measurable record of the full site — area, boundary, surface condition and the position of every built element — in a form that can be audited from a desk without visiting, and independent of whatever the field reported.

Orthomosaic + elevationMeasurable area and boundary Independent of field reportingRepeatable per cycle
LAYER 03

Satellite-based monitoring

The widest and cheapest layer, and the only one that scales to every asset at once. High-resolution imagery is read against the asset boundary to confirm existence, extent and change over time — without anyone going anywhere. It covers the long tail that drones and field visits will never reach, and keeps the register current as imagery refreshes.

Whole-portfolio coverageChange detection over time Refreshes with imageryNo field mobilisation
Why it holds up

Built to be checked.

Evidence, not assertions

Every status change carries a geo-tagged photograph, a timestamp and a named user. A figure on a dashboard can be opened down to the image behind it.

Accountability that resolves

Pendency is attributed to a named owner and escalates upward on a schedule. Nothing sits unowned, and nothing resets itself quietly at the week’s end.

Reconciled to the record

The asset register is matched against the authoritative source data on reference codes and names, so findings land in the existing workflow rather than beside it.

Capture that cannot be faked

Gallery upload is disabled, capture is confined to a geo-fence around the asset, and identity is one-time-password only. There is no path for a photograph taken somewhere else, at another time, to enter the record.

Independent corroboration

The drone and satellite layers are not reported by the people being measured. Where they disagree with the field record, the disagreement is the finding.

Exports at every level

District, block, site or single asset — whatever is on screen downloads in the same structure, so the figures in a review meeting and the figures in the system are the same figures.
