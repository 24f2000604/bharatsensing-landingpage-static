How It Applies

From pier base to deck soffit.

SuryaGbSAR mounts on an adjoining abutment, pillar or roadside station and scans the entire structure at full bandwidth - day and night, without affecting traffic.

🏗️

Deck deflection under load

Resolve mid-span deflection in real time against live traffic - validate WIM data and flag outlier truck events that structurally matter.

🧱

Pier & abutment settlement

Continuous sub-millimeter tracking of pier settlement and abutment rotation - particularly post-monsoon and during scour-prone seasons.

🎯

Cable & stay tension

Frequency-domain analysis from radar amplitude reveals cable-stay tension drift and localised corrosion-driven stiffness loss.

🌊

Scour & flood response

Rapid post-flood scans quantify residual deformation and inform go/no-go reopening decisions.

🚛

Load-posting validation

Verify whether reduced load postings are actually preventing fatigue on critical bridges.

📑

MoRTH compliance reports

Auto-generated monthly Health Index reports in MoRTH Circular-3/2021 format.
