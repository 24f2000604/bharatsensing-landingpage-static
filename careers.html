Open Roles

Where you fit.

Don't see an exact match? Write anyway - we hire slowly and well, and good people shape their own mandate here.

Senior Radar / RF Engineer · Bengaluru or Delhi

Own SuryaGbSAR's transceiver chain - design, simulation, integration, field validation. 8+ years in Ku/X-band radar, SAR or FMCW systems. Experience with ToT-style technology transfer a strong plus. Comfortable flying to a mine at 36 hours' notice.

Signal Processing & Interferometry Lead · Bengaluru

Own our InSAR / PSI pipeline end-to-end. Atmospheric correction, unwrapping, deformation inversion. Strong Python + C++, HPC / GPU experience preferred. PhD or 5+ years in remote-sensing DSP ideal.

ML Engineer · Failure & Anomaly Detection · Remote India

Build the ML layer that turns radar time-series into failure-signature predictions. Time-series, anomaly, self-supervised. Domain curiosity for geotech and structural engineering required.

Manufacturing & Supply-Chain Lead · Pune / NCR

Stand up our first indigenous radar production line. Own BoM localisation, vendor qualification, and DGAQA-style QMS. 10+ years in precision electronic manufacturing.

Field Operations Manager · Central India

Run deployments across mines and dams in Odisha, Chhattisgarh, Telangana, MP. Site surveys, installation, handover, customer training. 6+ years in mining or dam-site instrumentation.

Enterprise Sales · PSU / Infrastructure · Delhi

Own relationships with CIL subsidiaries, State WRDs, NHAI, NHPC. Navigate GeM, L1-tender and EoI processes. Ex-military or ex-PSU background welcomed.

Geotechnical Analyst · NOC · Bengaluru or Dhanbad

Triage alerts at our 24×7 Monitoring-as-a-Service NOC. Masters in geotechnical or structural engineering. You will be the first human eye on every anomaly.
