Why Radar

The only sensor that
sees an entire dam at once.

Legacy dam monitoring relies on a patchwork of survey prisms, pendulums, piezometers and manual inspections. GbSAR provides a remote, dense, always-on layer that complements every one of them.

🏗️

Crest & face

Hundreds of thousands of virtual measurement points across the entire dam face without drilling, bolting, or pausing operations.

🪨

Abutments & hill slopes

Early detection of hill-slope movement around abutments - historically a major source of overtopping and breach risk.

🌊

Downstream toe

Seepage-driven settlement and heave at the toe surfaces in the interferogram before any surface symptom is visible.

🧭

Post-seismic response

Rapid re-baselining after seismic events to quantify residual deformation and inform spillway operation decisions.

📊

Integration with DHARMA

Feeds into CWC's Dam Health and Rehabilitation Monitoring Application for regulatory compliance and benchmarking.

🛰️

Complements satellite InSAR

Ground radar scans multiple times per hour - satellite passes are every 6-12 days. Together they give seasonal + live coverage.
